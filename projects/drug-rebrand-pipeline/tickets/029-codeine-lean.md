# Ticket 029 — Codeine → lean / promethazine-codeine cough syrup

## Demon identity

**Lean** (also called purple drank, sizzurp, dirty Sprite) — recreational use of promethazine-codeine prescription cough syrup mixed with soda. Cultural emergence in Houston hip-hop scene 1990s (DJ Screw, Big Moe), broader cultural recognition through 2000s and 2010s rap music. Multiple high-profile overdose deaths (Pimp C 2007, others).

## Medicine identity

**Promethazine with codeine** (and standalone codeine products — Tylenol with Codeine #2, #3, #4). FDA-approved cough suppressants and analgesics. Codeine itself has been a prescribed analgesic for over 150 years (isolated from opium 1832 by Robiquet). Codeine is Schedule II as a single agent, Schedule III/V in combination products. Many codeine combination products have been discontinued or moved up in scheduling in recent years.

## Arc type

`medicine-to-street` arc primarily. Codeine cough syrup was the medicine first; lean is recreational misuse of the medicine. Doesn't fit the project's primary "demon-to-medicine" pattern, but Jason explicitly wanted both street-and-medicine-name-recognized cases.

## Chemistry hypothesis

**Identical** for the actual codeine content. Lean is recreational *use* of medicine, not a separate molecule. The promethazine adds sedation and creates the characteristic lean experience, but the codeine is unchanged.
