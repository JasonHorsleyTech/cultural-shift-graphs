# Ticket 009 — Psilocybin → psilocybin therapy

## Demon identity

Psilocybin (magic mushrooms, shrooms, boomers). Schedule I in the US since 1968 (initially the chemical) / 1970 (CSA). Cultural framing has been "hippie drug" more than "scary scourge" — never reached heroin/crack-level demonization. Decriminalized in several US cities since 2019 (Denver, Oakland, etc.); legalized for therapeutic use in Oregon (Measure 109, 2020) and Colorado (Prop 122, 2022).

## Medicine identity

**Psilocybin-assisted therapy** — no commercial brand yet as of 2026. Compass Pathways' COMP360 (synthesized psilocybin) is in Phase 3 trials for treatment-resistant depression. Usona Institute has separate trials. Oregon and Colorado have state-level service-center frameworks operating under the medical-supervision rubric.

## Arc type

`street_to_medicine`. Active rebrand in progress — not yet FDA approved as of research date. Confirm current status.

## Chemistry hypothesis

**Identical.** Synthetic psilocybin (COMP360 and others) is the same molecule as plant-derived psilocybin. Like MDMA-AT, the rebrand mechanism is purity + protocol + context, not chemistry.

## Caveats

- Like MDMA-AT, psilocybin therapy is inseparable from the therapy protocol. The medicine isn't just the molecule.
- Decriminalization, state legalization, and FDA approval are three different rebrand vectors moving in parallel. Track all three.
- The "shrooms" demonization was always lower-key than heroin/meth — flag that the demon-era intensity here is weaker, which affects the lag-and-success metrics.
