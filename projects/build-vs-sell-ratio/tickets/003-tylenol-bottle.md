# Ticket 003 — Tylenol bottle

## Product

Bottle of Tylenol (acetaminophen).

## Parent company

Kenvue Inc. (NYSE: KVUE) — spun off from Johnson & Johnson in 2023 as the standalone consumer-health business.

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Kenvue Self Care segment.** Tylenol is one of the segment's flagship brands (alongside Motrin, Nicorette, Zyrtec, Sudafed). Use segment revenue and operating income; apply parent advertising allocation.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Kenvue FY2024 10-K** — segment reporting, advertising expense (typically broken out for FMCG), R&D.
2. **Kenvue investor day presentation** for segment-level marketing intensity discussion.
3. **Kantar Media** for Tylenol-specific US ad spend.
4. **IRI / Nielsen panel data** if available for category benchmarks (probably paywalled — skip if not accessible).

## Specific caveats for this product

- Tylenol has had brand crises (1982 tampering, 2010 recalls) — heavy ongoing trust-marketing investment. Expect sell% on the higher side.
- OTC drug R&D is lower than Rx; mostly formulation tweaks and packaging innovation. R&D might be 1-3% of revenue.
- Kenvue is recently public (2023) — if FY2024 financials look unusual due to spinoff transition costs, note this and consider using the most-normalized figures available.
- Pharmaceutical retailer rebates and trade promotion are common — ensure they aren't double-counted.
