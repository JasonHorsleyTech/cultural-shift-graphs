## Specific caveats for this product

- Lululemon has very high gross margin (55%+) — small COGS relative to revenue. This will compress the build side.
- Owned retail stores (650+ globally) → sell per rubric. Material number.
- Brand-marketing investment historically modest as % of revenue (community/instructor model rather than TV) but rising.
- DTC e-commerce is large fraction — counts as distribution → build.
- Lululemon is a deliberately included "premium athleisure" data point. Expect sell-heavy on rubric.
- Capex moderate — supply chain investments, store buildouts.
