# Ticket 010 — Bud Light 6-pack

## Product

A 6-pack of Bud Light.

## Parent company

AB InBev (NYSE: BUD; primary listing Brussels).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**AB InBev North America segment** (where Bud Light's volume is concentrated). AB InBev publishes detailed segment data.

## Source hierarchy

1. **AB InBev 2024 Annual Report (20-F equivalent)** — segment revenue, EBIT, sales & marketing expense (broken out separately for AB InBev — this is a beer-industry norm).
2. **AB InBev investor materials** for North America commentary specifically (Bud Light has been highly visible due to 2023 Mulvaney controversy and volume decline).
3. **Beer Marketer's Insights / Beverage Digest** for category data.
4. **Kantar Media** for Bud Light specific ad spend.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Beer S&M expense is high — typically 25-35% of net revenue for big beer. This is genuinely sell-heavy (TV, sports sponsorships, sampling).
- 2023 Bud Light boycott / volume crash will have distorted FY2023 financials; FY2024 may show partial normalization. Note which year's data is used and whether it's "normalized."
- Beer is sold via three-tier distribution in the US (brewer → distributor → retailer). AB InBev's books are the brewer step only — distributor markup invisible. Same parent-vs-can issue as Coke but milder (distribution markup smaller).
- Capex is meaningful — breweries, packaging lines, kegs.

## Output format

`results/010-bud-light.md` per spec. Methodology notes should mention the FY2023 distortion if FY2024 data is still settling.

## What "good data" looks like

- Segment financials cited.
- Build_pct probably 50-65% — beer is genuinely marketing-heavy.
- Confidence: **medium-to-high**.
