# Bayonetta

## Character Summary
- **Source work:** Bayonetta franchise (PlatinumGames), debuting with Bayonetta (2009, PS3 / Xbox 360, published by Sega) and continuing across Bayonetta 2 (2014, Wii U, published by Nintendo), Bayonetta 3 (2022, Switch), and the prequel Bayonetta Origins: Cereza and the Lost Demon (2023). Director Hideki Kamiya, character designer Mari Shimazaki, character modeler Kenichiro Yoshimura. Per ticket instruction, the canonical reference is the **2009 original through Bayonetta 2 (2014)** — the period during which Bayonetta's signature male-fantasy iconicity was established and stabilized, and prior to the canonical romantic resolution with Luka in Bayonetta 3 (2022). The character's real name is **Cereza**; "Bayonetta" is the moniker she uses in her Death Dealer–equivalent role as one of the last surviving Umbra Witches.
- **Audience:** male-targeted
- **Decade slot:** 2010s
- **Cultural reach proxy:** The Bayonetta franchise has sold approximately **7 million units worldwide** across the four core titles (Bayonetta 1: 3.66M, Bayonetta 2: 1.51M, Bayonetta 3: 1.07M, plus the Origins prequel and various ports/re-releases — VGChartz / Video Game Sales Wiki cumulative reporting). Bayonetta (2009) was widely regarded as a defining title of the late-2000s action / character-action genre — recurringly cited in retrospective rankings as one of the greatest action games of all time (Edge, IGN, Polygon, GameSpot best-of-decade lists). Bayonetta 2 (2014) holds a Metacritic score of 91 and was Game Informer's Game of the Year 2014. The character herself is consistently placed on "sexiest video game characters" / "best video game heroines" / "iconic female protagonists" lists from IGN, GameSpot, Complex, Kotaku, Polygon throughout the 2010s and 2020s. Bayonetta is a defining male-fantasy iconography reference for late-2000s/2010s action gaming alongside Lara Croft (1996), Tifa Lockhart (1997), and 2B (2017) — but distinct in design philosophy (deliberately tall, lean, non-buxom, with the load-bearing fantasy projection on legs / height / sexual confidence rather than buxom-AND-slim body composition). Continuous top-tier cosplay status at conventions 2009–present.
- **Canonical version used:** Per ticket instruction, Bayonetta from the 2009 original through Bayonetta 2 (2014). Per the franchise canon (Bayonetta Wiki / Fandom canonical timeline, primary source game material): **Cereza was born December 19, 1411 in Vigrid** to the Umbra Witch Rosa and the Lumen Sage Balder — a forbidden union that broke an ancient tenet between the two clans and inadvertently triggered the Clan Wars. She was sealed during the Witch Hunts and remained sealed for ~500 years, awakening in the present-day setting of the 2009 game; she canonically presents as **mid-twenties physically** despite the ~600-year lore age. Following the dataset convention used for Selene (ticket 038, ~620 years), Black Widow (ticket 010, age drift), Wonder Woman (ticket 020, immortal), and Wanda Maximoff (ticket 012), the joint is computed using the 20–28 base population framing because the male-fantasy projection treats her as the archetypal "young attractive adult woman" regardless of canonical numerical age. Canonical design particulars per ticket and primary sources: tall (canonical natural-standing height ≈ 6'0"–6'1.6", stretched to ~8 ft with the iconic hair-heel silhouette), lean / slim figure, deliberately **non-buxom** per Kamiya's documented design statement ("avoided giving her large breasts and cleavage, feeling that normal sized breasts were adequate"), beehive hair, narrow rectangular spectacles (Kamiya's signature design instruction "to give her a 'sense of mystery and intelligence'"), bodysuit formed from her own hair (revealed during Wicked Weave attacks as her clothing dematerializes mid-combat).

The negative correlations (attractiveness↔single, attractiveness↔not-girly) push toward "more rare than naive product." For the FICTIONAL Bayonetta stack as written, the negative-correlation effect modestly DOMINATES because the supernatural-witch archetype is constructed precisely on the violation of real-world tradeoffs between conventional attractiveness and tomboy/warrior-coded interest profiles. Net: roughly **1.3x rarer** than the naive product. Same magnitude adjustment as Selene (ticket 038), Lara Croft (ticket 026), Black Widow (ticket 010), Wonder Woman (ticket 020).

No new entries required for this ticket.
- **Correlation adjustments:** medium-low — the directional claim that the fictional Bayonetta stack violates real-world negative correlations between attractiveness and tomboy/warrior-coded interests is well-supported in mate-market literature, but the exact magnitude (~1.3x net rarer) is judgment, not a measured joint distribution. Bracketing of [5e-5, 5e-4] is wide for that reason.
- **Cool Girl exclusion:** high confidence in the NOT-counted call. Bayonetta's smug-dominant-theatrical characterization is one of the cleanest counter-examples to Cool Girl coding in the dataset — she is *aggressive* about NOT being warm-low-friction-supportive, which is a more emphatic exclusion than even Lara Croft's aristocratic-aloof or Selene's cold-vampire characterization.
- **High-SOI inclusion:** high confidence in the COUNTED call. Bayonetta is the canonical high-SOI archetype on the male-target side of this dataset — her canonical sexual conduct fully aligns with her visual coding, distinguishing her from Jessica Rabbit (ticket 044, married-monogamous despite visual flirtation) and Daisy Duke (ticket 034, innocent despite visual flirtation), both of whom had high-SOI EXCLUDED. - **Methodological-asymmetry caveat:** **high relevance, medium confidence reduction.** Bayonetta is a master combatant (Bullet Arts virtuoso, four-pistol gunfighter, demon-summoner, time-manipulator, Wicked-Weave caster), with ~600 years of supernatural existence, family-unencumbered orphan status, and supernatural-tier physical capabilities. A symmetric-trait-list analysis would migrate Bayonetta substantially leftward on the rarity axis (see Notes / Edge Cases). Same caveat structure as Black Widow / Wonder Woman / Ciri / Lara Croft / 2B / Selene.

## Notes / Edge Cases

- **Methodological asymmetry — central caveat for Bayonetta:** Bayonetta is a master combatant across multiple unrelated mastery domains: Bullet Arts virtuoso (four-pistol gunfighting including foot-mounted pistols), master close-combatant (hand-to-hand and hair-magic), master demon-summoner (Infernal Demons including Madama Butterfly, Gomorrah, Phantasmaraneae), time-manipulator (Witch Time), elite practitioner of the Wicked Weave / Climax Brake / Umbran Witch arts. Plus ~600 years of supernatural existence, family-unencumbered status (mother killed in the Witch Hunts, father canonically deceased / antagonist by Bayonetta 1), and supernatural-tier physical capabilities. **The male-target trait list captures NONE of these as universal-desirable filters** because the project's premise is that male-target media doesn't typically *stack* skills onto female love interests as universal-desirable sliders the way female-target media stacks them onto male leads. If a SYMMETRIC trait-list analysis were applied — counting expert-combatant ~1e-3 (per the base-rates.md "expert combatant" entry from ticket 017), top-quintile fitness ~0.20 (supernatural-enhanced), family-unencumbered ~0.05 (orphaned by the Witch Hunts), plausibly multilingual ~0.03–0.20 (~600 years of European-supernatural existence), and arguably IQ ≥130 ~0.02 (tactical mastery + Umbran-Witch high-rank standing) — Bayonetta would gain roughly 4–5 additional rarity multipliers and her joint probability would collapse by **3–6 orders of magnitude** to ~1e-9 to ~1e-11. This would migrate her toward the rarest tier of the entire dataset, comparable to mid-pack female-target heartthrobs like Bridgerton-lead, Edward Cullen, Aragorn, or Rhysand. The plan's call (asymmetric trait lists) is taken because the male-target *audience* does not appear to stack those traits as universal sliders — Bayonetta is a male-fantasy figure first because she is hot-AND-tall-AND-leggy-AND-smug-AND-aggressively-sexual-AND-witch-coded, second because she can outshoot demons and rewrite local time. The math captures the sliders that actually drive male-fantasy projection. **Flag this asymmetry in the final-graph annotations.** Same structural caveat as Black Widow / Wonder Woman / Ciri / Lara Croft / 2B / Tifa / Selene — Bayonetta's symmetric-analysis stack is comparable in rarity to Lara Croft's (most heavily skill-stacked male-target character in the dataset) once the supernatural skill accumulation is counted.

- **Age-window-but-actually-immortal:** Per ticket convention (and following Selene ticket 038 / Black Widow ticket 010 / Wonder Woman ticket 020 / Wanda Maximoff ticket 012), Bayonetta's canonical 600-year age is treated as fantasy framing — the male-fantasy projection runs through the visual-young-adult-woman channel regardless of the lore numerical age. A reviewer who insists on the 20–28 age-window filter being violated would zero out the entire calc (Bayonetta is ~600, not 20–28). The plan's convention is to treat fantasy / supernatural-longevity framing as personality and compute the joint against the 20–28 base population. Flagged for completeness; does not invalidate the joint estimate.

- **Single-status interpretation across the franchise run:** Bayonetta is canonically single across the 2009 / 2014 reference window (Luka's romantic interest is unrequited, dismissed via physical kicks and verbal brush-offs). The romantic relationship with Luka is canonically confirmed only in Bayonetta 3 (2022), which is outside the ticket-specified reference window. A reviewer who reads "post-Bayonetta-3 canonical romance with Luka" as the load-bearing canonical status would zero out the single filter and pull the joint to ~3.8e-4 (~1 in 2,600) — still in the moderate-rare band but ~3x less rare. The single-default interpretation is consistent with how the male-fantasy projection was constructed across the load-bearing 2009 / 2014 iconography window, before the Bayonetta 3 canonical romantic resolution. Same precedent structure as Selene (ticket 038, single at the 2003 Underworld reference even though the Michael relationship develops later).

- **Cool Girl exclusion and the smug-dominant-warrior cluster:** Bayonetta's smug-dominant-theatrical characterization is structurally distinct from BOTH the warm-supportive Cool Girl warrior cluster (Black Widow / Wonder Woman) AND the cold-aristocratic-aloof warrior cluster (Lara Croft / Selene / Ciri). She is *aggressive* about NOT being warm-low-friction-supportive — she physically dismisses male suitors, treats male enemies as objects of theatrical contempt, and her dominant-sexual-confidence is performed *against* a Cool Girl coding rather than alongside one. Excluding Cool Girl drops one filter (factor of 0.10) from the stack, making Bayonetta roughly 10x less rare than she would be with Cool Girl included. This is the same arithmetic effect as for Lara / Selene / 2B / Ciri.

- **High-SOI inclusion and the Bayonetta-vs-Jessica-Rabbit / Bayonetta-vs-Daisy-Duke distinction:** Bayonetta is the cleanest example in the dataset of a high-SOI character — she shows aggressive performative sexuality across both 2009 and 2014 games, and (unlike Jessica Rabbit, ticket 044, who is canonically married-monogamous, and Daisy Duke, ticket 034, who is canonically innocent) her sexual coding is matched by her canonical sexual conduct. INCLUDING the high-SOI filter (×0.25) is the right call and pushes Bayonetta about 4x rarer than an otherwise-comparable witch-warrior with the high-SOI excluded. This is a precedent-setting inclusion for the male-target side of the dataset — most male-target heartthrobs in this project either have the high-SOI filter excluded (Selene, Lara, 2B, Wonder Woman, Yuna, Daisy, Jessica) due to canonical monogamy / asexuality / innocence, or have it included tacitly via the "alternative-coded sexually-active" archetype (CJ Parker, Tifa). Bayonetta is the canonical example of an action-protagonist female-character who openly carries the high-SOI filter as a load-bearing characterization beat.

- **DD+ exclusion via documented designer intent:** This is one of the few characters in the dataset where the body-composition call is supported by primary-source designer intent. Hideki Kamiya's documented statement ("avoided giving her large breasts and cleavage, feeling that normal sized breasts were adequate") is directly cited in Wikipedia / character-design coverage. The character's male-fantasy iconicity is deliberately routed through legs / height / fashion-model proportions / sexual confidence rather than through bust. Treating Bayonetta at the BMI marginal (0.22) rather than the joint (0.02) is the correct call and well-supported by primary-source evidence. Compare to Lara Croft (Toby Gard's accidental-150% breast-size apocrypha drove the buxom emphasis), Tifa (Tetsuya Nomura's deliberate buxom-design choice), and Jessica Rabbit (Richard Williams' "ultimate male fantasy" buxom-and-slim brief) — all three of which used the joint rate. Bayonetta is the deliberate counterexample.

- **Comparison to other 2010s male-target heartthrobs in the dataset:** Bayonetta's adjusted joint (~1.3e-4) sits in the moderate-rare band of the 2010s male-target distribution. Compared to 2B (ticket 032, with the BMI+DD+ joint and additional filters): 2B is ~10x rarer because of the body-comp joint. Compared to Daenerys Targaryen (ticket 040): comparable order-of-magnitude in calc rarity but distributed differently (Daenerys carries the no-kids+single+low-N+not-girly cluster without high-SOI). Compared to Rebecca from Cyberpunk: Edgerunners (ticket 042): Bayonetta is meaningfully less rare — Rebecca's stack carries the body-comp joint and additional aligned-hobby filters. Bayonetta is structurally one of the **less-stacked but more sexually-direct** 2010s male-target characters in the dataset, with the high-SOI inclusion being the distinguishing axis.

- **Comparison to Selene (ticket 038) — the closest structural analog:** Selene and Bayonetta are both gothic-supernatural-warrior female protagonists (vampire vs. witch) without Cool Girl coding, both clear top-quintile attractiveness + slim BMI marginal + single + no-kids + low-N + not-girly. The arithmetic difference between them is the high-SOI filter: Bayonetta carries it (canonical performative aggressive sexuality), Selene does not (canonical-monogamous arc with Michael). This single factor accounts for the ~6.5x rarity difference — Bayonetta at ~1.3e-4 vs. Selene at ~8.5e-4. Both characters share the gothic-supernatural-warrior + slim-athletic + single + no-kids + low-N + alternative-coded interest profile cluster; the high-SOI inclusion is the distinguishing axis. (This is the inverse of the Selene-vs-Lara comparison from ticket 038, where the body-composition joint was the distinguishing axis.)

- **Comparison to Lara Croft (ticket 026):** Lara at ~7.7e-5 (~1,000 matching) and Bayonetta at ~1.3e-4 (~1,800 matching) end up in similar rarity bands by canceling factors — Lara has the body-comp joint (0.02) but excludes high-SOI; Bayonetta has the BMI marginal (0.22) but includes high-SOI. The 11x reduction from the body-comp joint is roughly offset by the 4x reduction from including high-SOI, leaving the two characters at roughly comparable calc rarity despite emphasizing entirely different fantasy axes (Lara's buxom-aristocratic-asexual-explorer vs. Bayonetta's lean-supernatural-aggressively-sexual-witch). This is itself a finding for the project: distinct male-fantasy archetypes can converge on similar arithmetic rarity through different trait combinations.

- **Designer-team-context note (relevant for the project's hypothesis):** Bayonetta was designed by an all-female PlatinumGames team (Mari Shimazaki as character designer, with input from director Hideki Kamiya). The character's male-fantasy iconicity was thus constructed *by women, for a primarily male audience* — distinct from the typical authorial-male-Pygmalion pattern (Lara Croft / Toby Gard, Jessica Rabbit / Richard Williams, Tifa / Tetsuya Nomura, Lisa / John Hughes). Bayonetta is one of the canonical examples in the dataset of "male-fantasy projection constructed by a female creative team" — a methodological footnote rather than a calc adjustment, but worth flagging in the final-graph annotations alongside the other meta-textual reference points (Lisa, Jessica Rabbit). Some cultural-criticism literature reads this as empowering ("unapologetically feminine"), some reads it as internalized male-gaze projection — the project does not need to take a position, but should flag the authorial-team-context for narrative honesty.

- **Conservative reading vs. permissive reading.** I used the conservative reads on (a) the BMI marginal rather than the BMI+DD+ joint (designer intent supports), (b) including high-SOI (canonical performative sexuality supports), (c) the 2009/2014 single-status reference state rather than the post-Bayonetta-3 canonical romance with Luka, (d) excluding Cool Girl (smug-dominant characterization supports). A maximally-permissive reviewer who used the BMI+DD+ joint (treating the design as buxom despite Kamiya's documented intent), excluded high-SOI (treating the performative sexuality as personality rather than canonical conduct), and added an aligned-hobby filter at 0.05 (treating supernatural-witch-themed media-consumption as a niche-hobby alignment) would pull the joint to roughly 1.5e-7 (~1 in 6.7M), putting Bayonetta firmly in the deep-rare band alongside Mikaela / CJ Parker / 2B. The chosen filter set reflects the most-honest reading of her canonical 2009/2014 characterization rather than the male-fantasy-projection-on-the-character. The 100x range between the conservative and permissive readings is a normal signal for action-protagonist female characters in this dataset.
