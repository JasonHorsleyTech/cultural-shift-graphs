# Ticket 048 — Blue Bird school bus

## Product

A Blue Bird school bus (Vision, All American, or Micro Bird platforms — the iconic yellow school buses). Sold to school districts via dealer network. B2B / structural panel anchor — included as a "boring industrial monopolist" structural baseline.

## Parent company

Blue Bird Corporation (NASDAQ: BLBD).

## Panel

b2b

## Segment to use

**Blue Bird consolidated.** Blue Bird is a small-cap pure-play (revenue ~$1B-$1.5B range), and reports as a single operating segment — all bus manufacturing. Use consolidated income statement directly.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Blue Bird FY2024 10-K** (fiscal year ending Sep/Oct 2024). Look for: revenue, cost of goods sold, R&D expense (Blue Bird has been investing in EV school buses — should appear as a line), SG&A breakdown.
2. **Blue Bird FY2024 Annual Report and earnings call transcripts** for color on dealer support spend, EV transition R&D allocation, and any state/federal grant accounting (Blue Bird benefits from the EPA Clean School Bus Program — funds flow to school districts but drive Blue Bird sales).
3. **Trade press**: School Transportation News, Bus & Motorcoach News.
4. **Public peer comparison**: Thor Industries / REV Group (commercial buses) for cost-structure benchmarking, though neither is a perfect comp (Blue Bird is school-bus pure-play).

## Specific caveats for this product

- **Single segment makes this clean.** Unlike Boeing/Deere/GE, Blue Bird doesn't require segment carve-outs. The whole income statement IS the school bus business.
- **Dealer network is independent.** Sales go through ~50 independent Blue Bird dealers in North America. Dealer cost structure isn't on Blue Bird's books — note as methodology caveat (analogous to Deere).
- **Government procurement dynamics.** School districts buy via formal RFP processes — sales force activity is bid/proposal heavy rather than advertising heavy. Advertising as % of revenue is essentially zero. The "sell" line is dominated by sales force comp, dealer support, and proposal/marketing-collateral spend.
- **EV transition R&D.** Blue Bird has been investing in electric school buses (Vision EV, type-C and type-D EV models). R&D as % of revenue is meaningful and growing — count as build per rubric.
- **EPA Clean School Bus Program** is a major demand driver — federal funds go to districts to purchase EV/clean buses, including from Blue Bird. This affects revenue / order book but not cost structure classification.
- **Customer support / parts** — Blue Bird has an aftermarket parts business; modest relative to new-bus revenue. Counted as build.
