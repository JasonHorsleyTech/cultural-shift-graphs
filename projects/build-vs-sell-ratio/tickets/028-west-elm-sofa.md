## Segment to use

**West Elm brand within Williams-Sonoma.** Williams-Sonoma reports revenue by brand (Pottery Barn, West Elm, Williams Sonoma, Pottery Barn Kids/Teen) but expenses are mostly consolidated. Use brand revenue with consolidated expense ratios.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Williams-Sonoma FY2024 10-K** (fiscal year ending Feb 2025) — brand-level revenue, consolidated income statement.
2. **WSM investor presentations** for brand-level commentary.
3. **Furniture trade press** (Furniture Today) for category context.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Williams-Sonoma is vertically integrated retail (corporate-owned stores + DTC web). Stores → sell per rubric.
- WSM has been a quietly outperforming retail story — strong brand premium. Marketing is significant (catalog, digital, store).
- Sofas are imported (much from China/Vietnam) — COGS includes freight tariffs.
- WSM does some in-house design and "exclusive collections" — real R&D-equivalent (counted as build).
- Brand-level differentiation is hard since most cost lines are consolidated. Build_pct will be the WSM consolidated number — note this honestly.
