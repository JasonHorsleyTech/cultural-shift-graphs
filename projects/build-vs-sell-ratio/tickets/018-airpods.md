## Segment to use

**Apple Wearables, Home and Accessories category.** Includes AirPods, Apple Watch, HomePod, Beats, accessories. Allocate within category by AirPods' rough share (estimate from analyst reports — AirPods is the dominant single product within the category).

## Source hierarchy

1. **Apple FY2024 10-K** — Wearables/Home/Accessories revenue.
2. **Counterpoint Research / IDC** for AirPods unit volumes and ASP — needed to estimate AirPods' share of the category.
3. **Apple earnings call commentary** on Wearables margin (occasional).
4. **Kantar Media** for AirPods-specific advertising.

## Specific caveats for this product

- AirPods are estimated to be 60-70% of Wearables category revenue — use that estimate for share allocation.
- Wearables category gross margin is reportedly higher than iPhone — note.
- Lower advertising spend per dollar than iPhone.
- AirPods are essentially commoditized internally but sell on Apple ecosystem lock-in — interesting case where the brand premium is captured but advertising is moderate.
