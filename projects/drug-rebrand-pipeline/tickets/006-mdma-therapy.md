# Ticket 006 — MDMA → MDMA-assisted therapy

## Demon identity

MDMA (Ecstasy, Molly, X, E, rolling). Schedule I in the US since 1985 (DEA emergency scheduling after club use exploded). Cultural demonization peaked in the late 1990s — rave-culture moral panic, "Ecstasy deaths" tabloid coverage, Pee-Wee Herman-era DARE messaging.

## Medicine identity

**MDMA-assisted therapy** (sometimes branded under sponsor's program name; MAPS / Lykos Therapeutics is the leading sponsor). NDA submitted by Lykos for PTSD treatment in 2023. FDA rejected the Lykos application in August 2024 citing efficacy and methodology concerns. As of 2026, FDA approval is *not* yet in place — confirm current status. The medicine identity exists as a clinical-trial framework and (in some jurisdictions, e.g. Australia from July 2023) as a regulated prescribed treatment.

## Arc type

`both`. MDMA was *originally* a psychotherapy adjunct in the 1970s and early 1980s (used by therapists, called "Adam" in clinical circles) before recreational use exploded and it was scheduled. So the arc is medicine → demon → attempted medicine-again. One of the cleanest "spans both arcs" cases in the project.

## Chemistry hypothesis

**Identical.** MDMA-assisted therapy uses pharmaceutical-grade MDMA — the molecule, no enantiomer separation, just clinical-grade purity. The compound is 3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine in both cases. The rebrand mechanism here is *context and dosing protocol*, not chemistry.

## Source hierarchy

1. **MAPS / Lykos Therapeutics publications and trial data** — MAPP1 and MAPP2 phase 3 trials.
2. **FDA briefing documents** for the Lykos NDA, including the August 2024 rejection rationale.
3. **DEA scheduling history** — 1985 emergency scheduling, the 1988 final ruling.
4. **Australian TGA approval (July 2023)** — first national approval of MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD.
5. **Academic literature on MDMA pharmacology and therapeutic mechanism** — review papers on serotonin/oxytocin role.
6. **Cultural-history sources** for the 1970s "Adam" therapeutic use — Sasha Shulgin's PiHKAL, Greer & Tolbert clinical accounts.
7. **Survey data on public attitudes toward MDMA therapy** — searches for KFF, Pew, university research.

## Caveats specific to this drug

- The Lykos FDA rejection (August 2024) is recent and complicates the "successful rebrand" narrative. The rebrand is in flux as of research date — confirm current status.
- The therapy uses MDMA in a structured psychotherapy context (typically 3 dosing sessions, with prep and integration). The medicine identity is inseparable from the therapy protocol — this is unusual in pharma.
- "Molly" purity is famously poor — street MDMA is often cut or substituted. Worth noting that the medicine identity is *also* about getting actual pharmaceutical-grade molecule, not just relabeling.
- Public discourse on this rebrand has been unusually high (NYT, podcasts, etc.) — awareness % may be higher than for other Tier-B drugs.
