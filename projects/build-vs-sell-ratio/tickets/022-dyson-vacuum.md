# Ticket 022 — Dyson vacuum

## Product

A Dyson cordless stick vacuum (any current V-series).

## Parent company

Dyson Ltd. (private, UK-headquartered, manufacturing in Singapore/Malaysia/Philippines).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Dyson Group consolidated** (private). Dyson does file accounts at UK Companies House.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Dyson Technology Limited and Weybourne Group Limited filings at UK Companies House** — Dyson voluntarily files extensive financials in the UK. This is the primary source.
2. **Bloomberg / FT profiles of Dyson** for context on global revenue (UK filings are partial).
3. **Public peers** — iRobot (now BBY-owned but historical filings), SharkNinja (NYSE: SN) — for cordless vacuum cost-structure benchmarks.
4. **Trade press** (HFN, RetailDive) for category dynamics.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Dyson's UK filings cover a portion of the global business — note which entity is being measured.
- Dyson is genuinely R&D-heavy (own motor design, cyclone tech) — build will be elevated by R&D vs. peer hand-vac brands.
- Marketing is significant — Dyson runs heavy TV and digital, owns flagship retail stores.
- Dyson's flagship stores: retail real estate → sell per rubric. Material number for Dyson (50+ stores globally).
- Singapore HQ may distort some allocations — UK filings show a slice of global ops.

- Companies House filing year and entity name cited.
- Build_pct probably 60-75% — Dyson is genuinely build-heavy (R&D, motors) but has notable retail/marketing investment.
- Confidence: **medium** (Companies House is real data but may not capture full global ops).
