# Ticket 033 — Columbia Sportswear fleece

## Product

A Columbia Sportswear fleece jacket. Replaces the original "North Face fleece (VF Corp)" because Columbia is single-brand and gives cleaner segment data than VF, which reports a basket of brands (North Face, Vans, Timberland, etc.).

## Parent company

Columbia Sportswear (NASDAQ: COLM).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Columbia consolidated.** Single primary brand; single segment.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Columbia FY2024 10-K** — clean income statement, advertising disclosed.
2. **Columbia investor presentations** for brand-build commentary.
3. **NPD outdoor/active apparel** for category data.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Mix of wholesale and DTC (some owned outlet stores).
- Outdoor apparel category — moderate marketing intensity, less than premium athletic.
- Production contracted globally — COGS includes freight.

## Output format

`results/033-columbia-fleece.md` per spec.

## What "good data" looks like

- Income statement cited.
- Build_pct probably 70-82%.
- Confidence: **high**.
