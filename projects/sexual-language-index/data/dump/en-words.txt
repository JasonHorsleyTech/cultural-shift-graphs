**Notes on where this list is probably still incomplete:**

1. **AAVE and Black vernacular English** — this is likely the most undercounted register. Terms circulate fast, have short shelf lives, and are poorly documented in academic corpora. What's here scratches the surface.
2. **Regional British/Irish/Australian slang** — I have some (shag, bonk, minge, growler, fanny, norks, baps, knackers, minge) but regional variation (Scots, Welsh, Irish, Northern English dialects) is underrepresented.
3. **Gay community vernacular (especially older camp/polari)** — Polari (British gay argot) has its own sexual vocabulary (naff, trade, bona, cartes, lallies, basket) that is barely touched here.
4. **Prison and military slang** — highly specific registers with documented sexual vocabulary that is largely absent.
5. **Victorian and early 20th century slang** — terms documented in Farmer & Henley's *Slang and Its Analogues* and similar period dictionaries would add another layer.
6. **Non-English loanwords naturalized into English sexual slang** — beyond French and Yiddish, terms from Spanish, Italian, Japanese, and Creole traditions are underrepresented.
7. **Paraphilia/kink long tail** — the clinical DSM paraphilia list has ~50+ named conditions; only the most common are here.
8. **Portmanteau and neologism slang** — internet slang mutates yearly; 2024–2026 coinages are incompletely captured.
