# Ticket 021 — Nintendo overall

## Product

Nintendo's hardware + software business as a system. Restructured from "Switch" because Nintendo is a single integrated platform business — segment-level granular reporting isn't useful, and the Switch's economics depend on the software ecosystem.

## Parent company

Nintendo Co., Ltd. (TYO: 7974).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Nintendo consolidated.** Nintendo is essentially a single-segment company (video game platform) — consolidated financials are the right unit.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Nintendo FY2024 Annual Securities Report (fiscal year ending March 2024)** — full income statement, R&D as % of revenue, marketing.
2. **Nintendo investor presentations** for color on hardware margin and software attach rates.
3. **Tokyo Stock Exchange filings** for Japanese GAAP statement.
4. **Famitsu / NPD** for unit context.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Nintendo is famously profitable on hardware (unlike Sony's PlayStation), so the build/sell mix is different from PS.
- First-party software development (Mario, Zelda, Pokémon studios) is R&D → build.
- Marketing intensity is moderate — Nintendo relies heavily on first-party IP rather than mass advertising.
- Japanese GAAP "selling expenses" line includes some items US GAAP would put in COGS — note.
- FX translation needed (yen to USD) — state the year and rate used.
- Nintendo has unusually high cash balance and relatively low capex — note that capex is small for Nintendo (most mfg is contracted).

`results/021-nintendo-overall.md` per spec.

## What "good data" looks like

- Annual report financials cited with FX translation.
- Build_pct probably 70-85% — Nintendo is build-heavy (game development costs + hardware) but has lower marketing intensity than Sony.
- Confidence: **medium-to-high**.
