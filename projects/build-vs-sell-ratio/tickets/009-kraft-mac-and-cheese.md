# Ticket 009 — Kraft Mac & Cheese

## Product

A box of Kraft Mac & Cheese (the blue box).

## Parent company

Kraft Heinz (NASDAQ: KHC).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Kraft Heinz North America segment.** Same parent and segment as Heinz ketchup — but a different brand within the segment. The segment-level ratio will be the same as Heinz ketchup unless we have brand-level Kantar data to differentiate.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Kraft Heinz FY2024 10-K** — same source as ticket 008.
2. **Kantar Media** for Kraft Mac & Cheese specific US ad spend (this is the differentiator vs. Heinz ketchup at segment level).
3. **Kraft Heinz brand portfolio disclosures** in investor presentations.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Kraft Mac & Cheese is a "household staple" brand — typically lower advertising intensity than impulse/aspirational brands. Expect sell% lower than Oreos but similar to Heinz ketchup.
- This product overlaps significantly with ticket 008 at the segment level. **The differentiation will come from brand-level Kantar data.** If Kantar shows Kraft M&C ad spend is ~50% of Heinz ketchup ad spend per dollar of revenue, build% should be a few points higher. If they're identical, the two products will have nearly identical numbers — note this.
- Private-label competition from store brands has eroded the brand premium; some response in marketing investment.
