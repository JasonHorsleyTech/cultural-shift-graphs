# Simultaneous With Friends

For a bunch of everyday human acts (sneezing, peeing, pooping, farting, brushing teeth, crying, stubbing your toe, orgasming, having sex, dreaming, signing a contract, watching the end credits of a fantastic video game, riding in an ambulance, getting arrested, stuck in an elevator, hiccupping, having the same song stuck in your head, throwing up, getting locked out of the house) — how often does a moment occur where you and one of your ~50 closest friends are doing the exact same thing at the same time?

Two metrics per act:

1. **Time until simultaneous overlap with any one of your 50 closest friends.**
2. **Time until simultaneous overlap with your single best friend.**

The gap between (1) and (2) is the story. For most acts it should be ~50× — but for time-clustered acts (brushing teeth in the morning, sex in the evening) the friend-group compresses far more aggressively than the linear factor predicts.

Each act gets a full markdown research report: frequency, duration, time-of-day clustering, the math walkthrough, and the final number. The graph lets you expand any bar to see the full math.
