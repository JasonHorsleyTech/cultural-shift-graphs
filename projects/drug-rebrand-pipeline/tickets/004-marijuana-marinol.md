# Ticket 004 — Marijuana → Marinol / medical cannabis

## Demon identity

Marijuana (weed, pot, cannabis, grass, dope). Schedule I federally in the US (as of 2026, rescheduling to Schedule III is pending DEA review — note the current state). Cultural demonization peaks: Reefer Madness (1936), Nixon's War on Drugs (1971), Reagan's Just Say No (1980s). Began softening culturally in the 1990s with state-level medical legalization (CA Prop 215, 1996) and accelerated through the 2010s with recreational legalization in CO, WA (2012) and onward.

## Medicine identity

Multiple prescription paths:
- **Marinol** (dronabinol, synthetic THC). FDA approved 1985 for chemo nausea, 1992 for AIDS-related anorexia. Schedule III (less restrictive than the plant). AbbVie / Solvay.
- **Cesamet** (nabilone, synthetic cannabinoid analog). FDA approved 1985.
- **Epidiolex** (cannabidiol / CBD, purified from cannabis). FDA approved 2018 for Lennox-Gastaut and Dravet syndrome epilepsy. Jazz Pharmaceuticals (originally GW Pharmaceuticals).
- **Medical cannabis** at the state level (38+ US states as of 2026, exact count to confirm).

## Arc type

`street_to_medicine`. Multi-pronged rebrand: a pharmaceutical synthetic-THC arm (Marinol), a botanical CBD arm (Epidiolex), and a state-level "the plant itself is medicine" arm (medical cannabis programs).

## Chemistry hypothesis

- Marinol / dronabinol: **identical** to plant THC at the molecular level (Δ9-THC is Δ9-THC). The difference is purity, formulation, and source (synthetic vs. plant).
- Epidiolex / CBD: **identical** to plant CBD.
- Medical cannabis: **identical** — it's literally the plant.

This is rare for the project: a Tier-A culturally-recognized pair where the chemistry is also literally identical.

## Source hierarchy

1. **FDA approval letters** for Marinol (1985), Cesamet (1985), Epidiolex (2018).
2. **DEA scheduling history** for marijuana (Schedule I since 1970 Controlled Substances Act) and the pending Schedule III decision.
3. **State-level medical cannabis program timelines** — NCSL, ProCon, Marijuana Policy Project trackers.
4. **Gallup / Pew long-running marijuana support surveys** — these are gold for tracking awareness and approval over time.
5. **IQVIA / state cannabis program data** for prescription/registered-patient volume.
6. **Marinol vs. plant cannabis clinical efficacy literature** — important for the honesty check (Marinol underperforms whole-plant cannabis in many patients).

## Caveats specific to this drug

- Marinol has had famously low patient acceptance — the synthetic-THC-only experience is reported as less effective and more dysphoric than whole-plant cannabis. The "successful rebrand" framing should reflect this.
- Recreational legalization in many US states is a parallel rebrand path that bypasses the medical framing entirely. Mention but keep the project focused on the medical rebrand.
- Federal Schedule I status is the load-bearing tension here — the medicine and the plant are still legally distinct at the federal level. The rescheduling decision (when it lands) is a major data point.
- CBD-as-wellness-product (gummies, tinctures, all over Whole Foods) is a different rebrand path — note but stay focused on Epidiolex as the prescription identity.
