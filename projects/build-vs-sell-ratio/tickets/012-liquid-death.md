# Ticket 012 — Liquid Death water

## Product

A can of Liquid Death (the canned water brand).

## Parent company

Liquid Death Inc. (private, valued ~$1.4B at last raise in March 2024).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Whole company (private).** Liquid Death is single-brand, so segment isn't an issue.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Press releases and TechCrunch / Forbes profiles** for revenue scale (reported ~$263M revenue in 2023, ~$130M in 2022).
2. **Founder interviews and podcast appearances** (Mike Cessario has been candid about marketing spend ratios — heavy social/influencer/stunt marketing).
3. **Public peer cost structure** — Celsius, Monster, BodyArmor (now Coke-owned) — for triangulation of beverage cost ratios.
4. **PitchBook/Crunchbase** for funding-round disclosures that may include financial color.
5. **Industry estimates from Beverage Digest / NielsenIQ / IRI** for retail-scanner data.

## Specific caveats for this product

- **Heroically sell-heavy by design.** Founder is an ex-creative; the brand is a marketing-first construct. Founder has publicly said marketing is ~30%+ of revenue.
- Manufacturing is co-packed (Austrian water source, US-based canning partners). All build is contracted out — relatively thin internal "build" cost structure.
- No public filings — all numbers are estimates with explicit ranges.
- Profitability is uncertain (private startup); operating losses likely subsidize sell-side investment.
- This is a deliberate "very sell-heavy" data point on the chart.

## Output format

`results/012-liquid-death.md` per spec. Numbers are explicitly estimates; show ranges where appropriate (e.g., "build $20-40M").

## What "good data" looks like

- Triangulated revenue in the $200-300M range cited from multiple sources.
- Marketing intensity estimate of 25-40% of revenue with a citation (founder interview, etc.).
- Build_pct likely 35-55% — sell-heavy.
- Confidence: **low** — but valuable as the DTC/viral example.
