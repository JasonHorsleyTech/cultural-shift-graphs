# Ticket 013 — LSD → Delysid history + current therapy trials

## Demon identity

LSD (acid, lucy, blotter, tabs). Schedule I in the US since 1968. Cultural demonization peaks: late 1960s counterculture backlash, Nixon's War on Drugs, 1980s DARE messaging. Notably softer demonization than meth/heroin — closer to marijuana's "cultural misfit" framing than "destroyer of lives."

## Medicine identity

Two arcs to cover:
1. **Historical:** Sandoz marketed LSD as **Delysid** from 1947 to ~1965 for psychiatric research and self-experimentation by clinicians. ~40,000+ patients received LSD-assisted therapy in this era. Sandoz withdrew the drug in 1965 under pressure from counterculture concerns.
2. **Current:** Multiple LSD-assisted therapy trials are underway as of 2026 — MindMed (LSD for anxiety, generalized anxiety disorder), various Phase 2 work. No FDA approval yet. Compare to the more advanced MDMA-AT and psilocybin-AT pipelines.

## Arc type

`both`. LSD was originally a medicine (Delysid), got banned, and is now being re-medicalized. Spans both arcs cleanly.

## Chemistry hypothesis

**Identical.** Both Delysid and current trial LSD use the same molecule (lysergic acid diethylamide). The rebrand is, again, about purity + context + protocol, not chemistry.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Sandoz Delysid historical materials** — original prescribing information, marketing letters.
2. **Hofmann's papers** on LSD discovery (1938 synthesis, 1943 self-experiment).
3. **DEA scheduling history** — 1968 (chemical) / 1970 (CSA).
4. **MindMed Phase 2 LSD-anxiety trial registration and publications.**
5. **Academic literature** on the 1950s–60s psychiatric LSD use era — Stanislav Grof's work, Spring Grove studies.
6. **Survey research** on public awareness of LSD therapy.

## Caveats

- The 1950s–60s LSD therapy era is genuinely under-remembered. ~40,000 patients is substantial. The "first time" framing for current trials is misleading.
- Public awareness of "LSD as therapy" is probably moderate and rising, given media coverage. Find recent survey data.
- LSD-AT trials are behind MDMA-AT and psilocybin-AT in the regulatory pipeline. The current-status reporting matters.
