# Ticket 019 — Samsung 65" TV

## Product

A Samsung 65-inch QLED or similar TV.

## Parent company

Samsung Electronics (KRX: 005930).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Samsung Visual Display business** (within the Device eXperience or DX division). Samsung is a giant conglomerate; segment selection matters a lot.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Samsung Electronics 2024 Business Report (Korean Annual Report) and 20-F equivalent** — segment reporting at division level (DX, DS, etc.) and sub-segment for Visual Display.
2. **Samsung investor presentations** for marketing-spend color (Samsung is a top-3 global advertiser).
3. **Korea Investors Service (KIS)** or Bloomberg for parsed segment data.
4. **DisplaySearch / Omdia** for TV market context.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Samsung is a famously heavy advertiser globally — Olympic sponsorship, sports, retail co-marketing. Sell intensity is very high for a hardware company.
- Samsung Electronics also makes memory chips (DS division) — drastically different cost structure. Make sure to use the Visual Display sub-segment, not parent.
- Samsung's Korean accounting may differ from US GAAP in classification of "selling expenses" vs. "general admin." Note.
- Korean filings + 20-F translate to dollar terms — note the FX year.

## Output format

`results/019-samsung-tv.md` per spec.

## What "good data" looks like

- Visual Display segment financials cited in dollars at a stated FX rate.
- Build_pct probably 65-80%.
- Confidence: **medium** (segment data is detailed but classification may need translation).
