# Ticket 011 — Jack Daniel's bottle

## Product

A bottle of Jack Daniel's Old No. 7 Tennessee Whiskey.

## Parent company

Brown-Forman (NYSE: BF.B).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Brown-Forman consolidated.** The company is dominated by Jack Daniel's — single-product approximation works well. Jack accounts for ~60-70% of company revenue.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Brown-Forman FY2024 10-K** (fiscal year ending April 30). Single-segment company makes this clean.
2. **Brown-Forman investor presentations** for marketing-spend color (Brown-Forman discloses "advertising expenses" line).
3. **Distilled Spirits Council (DISCUS)** for category benchmarks.
4. **Kantar Media** for Jack Daniel's-specific advertising.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Spirits have very high gross margin (60-70%) but also very high marketing intensity (15-25% of revenue). The build/sell ratio will reflect that — both numerator and denominator sizable.
- Three-tier distribution applies (distiller → distributor → retailer) — same caveat as beer about parent-vs-bottle.
- Aging inventory (Jack ages 4+ years) shows up as a working-capital line, not COGS in the year of revenue. Don't double-count.
- Capex on barrels and warehousing is meaningful but spread over decades.
- Brown-Forman is one of the cleaner spirits comparables — single segment, single dominant brand.

## Output format

`results/011-jack-daniels.md` per spec.

## What "good data" looks like

- Disclosed advertising expense from 10-K cited directly.
- Build_pct probably 50-65% — spirits are marketing-heavy.
- Confidence: **high** (clean single-segment company).
