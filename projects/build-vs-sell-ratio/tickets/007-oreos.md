# Ticket 007 — Pack of Oreos

## Product

A standard pack of Oreos.

## Parent company

Mondelez International (NASDAQ: MDLZ).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Mondelez North America segment** OR overall consolidated (depending on what's cleanly available). Oreo is a global brand — the parent's overall ratio is reasonable; if North America segment data is granular enough, use it.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Mondelez FY2024 10-K** — segment reporting, advertising/marketing expense.
2. **Mondelez investor day** — Mondelez has discussed brand-by-brand marketing intensity for "power brands" (Oreo, Cadbury, Toblerone) at investor events.
3. **Kantar Media** for Oreo brand-specific ad spend.
4. **Statista** for cookie category data.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Mondelez's "power brand" strategy concentrates marketing on top brands — Oreo gets disproportionate ad investment. Brand-specific number should be higher than parent average.
- Trade promotion at supermarkets and convenience channels is significant. Some netted against revenue.
- Distribution: significant DSD operations in some markets, warehouse in others. Counted as build.
- Cocoa cost volatility (current run-up in 2024) may distort COGS relative to historical.

`results/007-oreos.md` per spec.

## What "good data" looks like

- Segment or category-level data with citations.
- Build_pct probably 55-70% (Oreos is more marketing-heavy than Cheerios but less than Coke).
- Confidence: **medium**.
