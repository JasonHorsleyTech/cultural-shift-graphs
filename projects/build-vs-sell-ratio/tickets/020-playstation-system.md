# Ticket 020 — PlayStation system (console + games)

## Product

The PlayStation system as a whole — PS5 console + first-party games + PlayStation Plus subscriptions. Restructured from "PS5" because the standalone console is sold near cost; the system economics are the honest unit. (See `research/angle-02.md` fatal break #2.)

## Parent company

Sony Group (NYSE: SONY).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Sony Game & Network Services (G&NS) segment.** This segment includes hardware, software (first- and third-party royalties), PlayStation Plus, PlayStation Network. It's the right unit of analysis.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Sony FY2024 Annual Report (fiscal year ending March 2025) and 20-F** — G&NS segment revenue, operating income, R&D, advertising.
2. **Sony investor day presentations** for G&NS color.
3. **NPD Group / Circana** for PS5 unit and software volume context.
4. **Sony earnings calls** for hardware margin commentary (Sony has discussed PS5 hardware reaching breakeven/profit).

## Specific caveats for this product

- Hardware (PS5) is sold near cost; the segment as a whole is profitable due to software/royalties/PS+. Build/sell at the segment level reflects the system economics, which is the intent.
- Sony classifies sales force / marketing differently than US peers — note translation.
- First-party game studio costs (Naughty Dog, Insomniac, etc.) are R&D in the rubric → build.
- PlayStation Plus customer support is genuinely build (without it the service degrades).
- This entry is restructured deliberately — the methodology notes must explain why we're using the segment, not the SKU.

`results/020-playstation-system.md` per spec. **Methodology notes must explain the SKU-to-system restructuring.**

- G&NS segment financials with citations.
- Build_pct probably 75-85% — heavy build (hardware mfg, game studios), moderate sell.
- Confidence: **medium-to-high**.
