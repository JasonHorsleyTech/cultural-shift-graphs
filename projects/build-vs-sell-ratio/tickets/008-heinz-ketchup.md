# Ticket 008 — Heinz ketchup

## Product

A bottle of Heinz tomato ketchup.

## Parent company

Kraft Heinz (NASDAQ: KHC).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Kraft Heinz North America segment** (or relevant geographic segment if global). Heinz is a flagship brand; segment ratio is a reasonable proxy.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Kraft Heinz FY2024 10-K** — segment reporting, advertising expense (broken out for KHC).
2. **Kraft Heinz earnings calls** for color on Heinz brand investment.
3. **Kantar Media** for Heinz US ad spend.
4. **Industry trade press** (Food Business News).

## Specific caveats for this product

- Kraft Heinz has been on a multi-year cost-cutting program (3G capital legacy) — advertising expense is structurally lower than peers as % of revenue. Note this; it makes Heinz look more build-heavy than it would under different management.
- Heinz has near-monopoly share in ketchup category in US — limits need for promotional spending.
- Co-packers and contract manufacturing are common in condiments — verify whether COGS includes outsourced production.
- Distribution: warehouse delivery dominant. Counted as build.
