# Ticket 034 — Rolex Submariner

## Product

A Rolex Submariner watch.

## Parent company

Rolex SA — privately held by the Hans Wilsdorf Foundation. Switzerland.

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Rolex SA consolidated.** No public filings. Use triangulation from Federation of the Swiss Watch Industry data and public peer cost structures.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Federation of the Swiss Watch Industry FH** — exports by price band, helps estimate Rolex unit volumes.
2. **Morgan Stanley / LuxeConsult Swiss Watch Industry Reports** (annual) — these explicitly estimate Rolex revenue (~$10-12B/yr 2023-2024).
3. **Swatch Group (SIX: UHR) and Compagnie Financière Richemont (SIX: CFR) annual reports** — public peers in luxury watches; provide cost-structure benchmarks for Swiss watch industry.
4. **Trade press** (Hodinkee, Watches of Switzerland investor commentary).

## Specific caveats for this product

- All numbers are estimates with explicit ranges — note this prominently.
- Rolex doesn't run heavy traditional advertising (no TV ads in many markets) but invests heavily in:
  - Tournament sponsorships (Wimbledon, F1, golf majors)
  - Brand ambassadors (federation watches, etc.)
  - Authorized dealer network and retail boutique support
- Production is in-house and capital-intensive (Geneva facilities). Build is genuinely substantial.
- Margins are famously high — gross margin estimated 50-70%.
- Watches of Switzerland (LSE) is a Rolex AD; their financials show retail markup but not Rolex's wholesale-to-retail relationship.

## Output format

`results/034-rolex-submariner.md` per spec. Numbers must be expressed as ranges with citations.

## What "good data" looks like

- Multiple cited triangulation sources.
- Estimates expressed with ranges (e.g., "build $X-Y B").
- Build_pct probably 50-65% — heavy build (in-house manufacturing) but heavy sell (sponsorships, AD network).
- Confidence: **low**.
