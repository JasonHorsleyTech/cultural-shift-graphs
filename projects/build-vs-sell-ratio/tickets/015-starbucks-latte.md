# Ticket 015 — Starbucks latte

## Product

A grande latte at Starbucks.

## Parent company

Starbucks Corporation (NASDAQ: SBUX).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Starbucks North America segment** (or consolidated, depending on cleanest data). About 50% of US locations are corporate-owned and 50% licensed; for North America, focus on the company-operated stores' economics.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Starbucks FY2024 10-K** — segment reporting, store-operating expenses, marketing expense (Starbucks discloses).
2. **Starbucks investor presentations** for store-level unit economics.
3. **Reqs of public competitors** (Dutch Bros, BROS) for fast-casual coffee benchmarks.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Same occupancy classification issue as Chipotle — counted as build (storefront is the production facility for the latte).
- Starbucks Rewards program is a major customer-loyalty / sell investment that's harder to classify than ad spend. Default to sell.
- Channel Development segment (CPG products in grocery, K-Cups via Nestlé licensing) is a separate business line — exclude or note.
- Licensed stores generate licensing revenue; the cost structure of licensed stores isn't on Starbucks' books. For consistency with our framing, focus on company-operated stores or consolidated.
- Higher marketing intensity than Chipotle (Pumpkin Spice campaigns, holiday cups, etc.).

## Output format

`results/015-starbucks-latte.md` per spec.

## What "good data" looks like

- Segment financials cited.
- Build_pct probably 75-85% — heavy build (in-store labor, supply chain), moderate sell (some advertising, loyalty programs).
- Confidence: **high**.
