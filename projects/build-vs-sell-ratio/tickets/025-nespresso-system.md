# Ticket 025 — Nespresso system (machine + pods)

## Product

The Nespresso system — espresso machine + capsule pods. Restructured from "Nespresso machine" because the standalone machine is sold thin/at-cost and the system economics live in pods. (See `research/angle-02.md` fatal break #2.)

## Parent company

Nestlé S.A. (SIX: NESN).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Nestlé Coffee category** (Nestlé groups by category and geographic zone). Nespresso is reported as a "globally managed business" (GMB) with some standalone disclosure. Use Nespresso GMB if available; otherwise the broader Coffee category.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Nestlé 2024 Annual Report** — Coffee category and Nespresso GMB disclosures. Nestlé reports advertising and marketing as a separate line.
2. **Nestlé investor day presentations** for Nespresso-specific color.
3. **Trade press** (Beverage Daily, Tea & Coffee Trade Journal) for category context.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Restructured deliberately — methodology notes must explain why we measure machine+pods together rather than just the machine SKU.
- Nestlé reports in CHF; FX translation needed.
- Nespresso operates owned retail boutiques (Champs-Élysées, NYC, etc.) — clear sell-side investment.
- Capsule manufacturing is automated and capex-light per pod, but high volume.
- Marketing intensity is high — George Clooney, sponsorships, retail presence. Sell-side is meaningful.
- Customer support (Nespresso Club, machine repair) is real → build per gut test.

## Output format

`results/025-nespresso-system.md` per spec. Methodology must explain SKU-to-system restructuring.

## What "good data" looks like

- Coffee category or Nespresso GMB financials cited from 2024 annual report.
- Build_pct probably 55-70% — the system is more sell-heavy than the standalone machine would look.
- Confidence: **medium**.
