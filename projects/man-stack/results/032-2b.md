# 2B (YoRHa No.2 Type B)

## Character Summary
- **Source work:** NieR: Automata (PlatinumGames / Square Enix, 2017) — PS4/Steam/Xbox/Switch action-RPG. Subsequently the NieR: Automata Ver1.1a anime adaptation (2023–2024, Aniplex / A-1 Pictures). 2B is the central female protagonist of NieR: Automata; canonical YoRHa android designation is "YoRHa No.2 Type B," with the in-game reveal that she is structurally a "Type E" (Executioner) covertly assigned to repeatedly delete her partner 9S whenever he discovers forbidden information about the YoRHa project.
- **Audience:** male-targeted
- **Decade slot:** 2020s (per heartthrobs.md — 2017 release with continued/peaked cultural footprint through the 2020–2024 anime adaptation, 10M+ unit sales milestone in 2025, and ongoing top-tier cosplay / fan-art / waifu-ranking presence)
- **Cultural reach proxy:** NieR: Automata sold **8M+ units by Feb 2024**, **9M+ by Dec 2024**, and **10M+ by 2025** per Square Enix shipment-and-digital reporting. The game is one of the best-selling Square Enix entries of its era and revived the broader NieR / Drakengard franchise. The Ver1.1a anime adaptation (2023–2024, Aniplex / A-1 Pictures) extended 2B's reach into mainstream anime audiences. 2B has been the **single most-cosplayed female video-game character of the late 2010s / early 2020s** by a meaningful margin (continuous top-1 placement at PAX, San Diego Comic-Con, Anime Expo cosplay aggregations 2017–2024), and has anchored continuous "best video game waifu" / "hottest female video game characters" rankings across IGN, GameRant, ScreenRant, GameSpot, TheGamer, and Anime News Network for nearly a decade. Yoko Taro (director) has openly stated in interviews that the character design was deliberately calibrated for fanservice and male-audience appeal ("she had to look pretty"). The fan-creation footprint (fan art, mods, doujinshi, AMV remixes, "Becoming as Gods" memes) is among the largest of any female video-game character in modern gaming history.
- **Canonical version used:** NieR: Automata (2017) 2B — the white-blindfolded, silver-haired, black-gothic-Lolita-combat-dress canonical design with white feathered cape, by character designer Akihiko Yoshida. Per the NieR: Automata official guide book, 2B's vital statistics are: height 168 cm (5'6", inclusive of heels), weight 148.8 kg (the high mass reflects the metal/synthetic-construction android frame, not human BMI), measurements B84–W56–H88 cm. The game's narrative places her at "approximately 3 years old" as an android by the climax, but the apparent age (the load-bearing male-fantasy projection per the ticket note "fantasy framing for age and physical perfection") presents as a young adult woman in the 20–28 visual band. The Ver1.1a anime version preserves the same canonical design.

  - Top-quintile attractiveness ↔ body-composition joint: positive halo (slim-and-busty body type already contributes to high attractiveness ratings in male-rating studies, partial overlap). Naive product OVERSTATES rarity by ~1.2–1.4x.
  - No-kids ↔ currently single: positive (~1.2x OVERSTATES).
  - Top-quintile attractiveness ↔ currently single: NEGATIVE in real world (more attractive women more likely partnered). Naive product UNDERSTATES rarity by ~1.2–1.4x.

Net adjustment: roughly **1.3–1.5x LESS rare** than naive product (positive halo dominates because the "Cool Girl + low-N + attractive + slim-and-buxom" cluster is the densest positive-halo configuration, and the negative-correlation effect is partly offset by the deliberate-fictional-violation framing — same structural pattern as CJ Parker minus the high-SOI factor).

This places 2B in the **rare end** of the male-target distribution — substantially rarer than Yuna (~660 matches, ticket 018) because 2B clears the body-composition JOINT (0.02) where Yuna only clears the BMI marginal (0.22), and slightly less rare than CJ Parker (~15 matches, ticket 016) and Mikaela Banes (~24 matches, ticket 008) because both of those characters add an additional 0.02-tier rarity factor (CJ adds high-SOI; Mikaela adds car-expertise) on top of the same Cool Girl + low-N + body-comp stack. 2B is structurally the **"Cool Girl + low-N + body-composition joint" archetype without the additional 0.25-tier high-SOI multiplier or the additional 0.02-tier expert-skill multiplier** — a clean test of how the body-composition joint by itself (no further skill or SOI stacking) places a character on the rarity axis. **Critically: this male-target-methodology number under-counts 2B's total stack — see Notes / Edge Cases for the symmetric-trait-list comparison, which would migrate her into the rare-skill-stacker band alongside Lara Croft.**

## Notes / Edge Cases

- **Android fantasy framing — the central methodological note:** Per ticket instruction, 2B is "Android — fantasy framing for age and physical perfection." She is canonically ~3 years old as a YoRHa unit (manufactured shortly before the game's events) but the apparent age and the load-bearing male-fantasy projection treat her as a young-adult-female in the 20–28 visual band. The android frame ALSO functions as a fantasy-framing device for the body-composition joint (the literal explanation for the canonical B84–W56–H88 proportions on a 168-cm frame is "she was manufactured this way," not anthropometric realism), and for the personality stack (the literal explanation for the dense Cool-Girl-low-N halo is "YoRHa programming forbids emotional expression," not Big-5 percentile accident). The methodology nonetheless applies the standard human base rates because the male-fantasy projection is on the projected-as-human reading of the character — fans cosplay her as a hot stoic girl with prominent bust, not as a robot. This is the same convention applied to other fantasy-framed characters (Wonder Woman as Amazonian, Yuna as summoner, Ciri as Elder-Blood-mage, Black Widow as super-spy): the audience-facing projection is on the human-readable traits, not the in-fiction explanation.

- **Methodological asymmetry — same as Black Widow / Wonder Woman / Lara Croft / Ciri:** 2B is canonically a top-tier YoRHa Executioner-class combat android with master sword skill, dual-wielding capability, expert ranged-weapon and pod-system proficiency, and near-superhuman physical capability (jumps, evasion, durability). If the male-target trait list MIRRORED the female-target list (counting expert-combatant status, top-quintile fitness, multiple expert skills), 2B would stack 3–4 additional rarity multipliers and her joint would collapse by 3–5 orders of magnitude — placing her below Christian Grey / Edward Cullen on the rarity axis. The plan's call (asymmetric trait lists) is taken because male-target media doesn't typically *stack* skills onto female love interests as universal-desirable sliders the way female-target media stacks them onto male leads. For 2B specifically, this asymmetry is testable: her combat capability IS part of her appeal (the action gameplay is core to the game's identity), but the question is whether the median male audience reads it as universal-desirable (slider up = better) or as characterization (a feature of her specific archetype). The plan's call is the latter, and 2B's "deadly combat android" iconicity is best read as the *characterization* of the YoRHa-soldier archetype rather than a separately stackable universal slider. **Same methodological asymmetry note as Black Widow ticket 010, Wonder Woman ticket 020, Lara Croft ticket 026, and Ciri ticket 022 — flagged here for final-graph annotations.** A symmetric-trait-list calculation would migrate 2B into or below the Lara Croft band (i.e. 5+ orders of magnitude rarer in the symmetric reading).

- **Body-composition joint (0.02) call is the load-bearing arithmetic decision:** The single most consequential arithmetic decision separating 2B's stack (~6e-6 naive) from Yuna's stack (~7e-5 naive) is the body-composition factor — 0.02 joint vs. 0.22 marginal, ~10x difference.
