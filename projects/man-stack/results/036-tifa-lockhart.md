# Tifa Lockhart

## Character Summary
- **Source work:** Final Fantasy VII (1997, Square / PlayStation / PC), the original Squaresoft JRPG that defined the late-1990s console-RPG era. Reprised across the Compilation of Final Fantasy VII — Advent Children (2005 CGI film), Crisis Core: Final Fantasy VII (2007 PSP), Dirge of Cerberus (2006), and the modern remake trilogy: Final Fantasy VII Remake (2020 PS4/PS5), Crisis Core: Reunion (2022), and Final Fantasy VII Rebirth (2024 PS5). Per the project's anchor-on-original convention, the Squaresoft 1997 PSX Tifa is the canonical reference for the 1990s decade-slot.
- **Audience:** male-targeted
- **Decade slot:** 1990s
- **Cultural reach proxy:** Final Fantasy VII (1997) sold **~14 million units worldwide** across PSX/PC/Steam/iOS/Switch/etc. (Square Enix cumulative reporting), making it one of the best-selling PlayStation 1 games of all time and the title that brought JRPGs into the Western mainstream. The FFVII Remake (2020) sold **~7+ million units** as of 2024 and Rebirth (2024) added another several million. Tifa is consistently in the **top 3–5 of "best Final Fantasy characters" / "best video game heroines" / "top JRPG waifu" rankings** across IGN, GameSpot, Polygon, Famitsu, Kotaku, Den of Geek, and Anime News Network — recurringly anchored against Aerith as the dataset-defining "two best girls" of FFVII for nearly 30 years. Continuous top-tier cosplay status from 1997 to present; her white tank top + black miniskirt + suspenders + leather gloves + long hair + boots silhouette is one of the most-recognizable video-game character designs in history. She has been a recurring playable / featured character in Kingdom Hearts (Cloud's "girlfriend" appearance), Dissidia Final Fantasy, World of Final Fantasy, Theatrhythm, and as a long-running male-fantasy reference point in gaming culture broadly.
- **Canonical version used:** Final Fantasy VII (1997) Squaresoft Tifa at age 20 (born May 3, 1987 per Final Fantasy VII Ultimania Omega; FFVII timeline places main events in May–December 2007 → age 20 at game start, exactly at the lower edge of the project's 20–28 male-target base population). The 1997 PSX polygonal model + officially-published Ultimania character art is the canonical reference. The 2020 FFVII Remake redesign (athletic build with sports-bra underlayer added; bust toned down per producer Yoshinori Kitase's deliberate ratings-board adjustment per his published 2020 Famitsu interview) is a tonal walk-back of the most-exaggerated body-composition aspect of the original — but per project anchor convention, the load-bearing reference is the original 1997 release.

## Joint Probability

- **Method:** filter (male-target stack)
- **Naive product of marginals:**
  0.20 × 0.02 × 0.34 × 0.70 × 0.10 × 0.25 × 0.25
  = (0.20 × 0.02) = 0.004
  × 0.34 = 0.00136
  × 0.70 = 9.52e-4
  × 0.10 = 9.52e-5
  × 0.25 = 2.38e-5
  × 0.25 = **5.95e-6**
  (≈ 1 in 168,000, applied within a base population of US women aged 20–28)
  Note: the body-composition factor (0.02) is already a joint rate for BMI 18.5–22 AND DD+, NOT a product of marginals. Multiplying separate marginals would have given a rarer naive estimate by ~5–10x.
- **Correlation adjustment:** Mixed-direction adjustments, with the body-composition joint already applied at the joint rate (so no further BMI×bust adjustment needed). Remaining pairs:
  - Top-quintile attractiveness ↔ body-composition joint: positive halo (slim-and-busty body type already contributes to high attractiveness ratings in male-rating studies, partial overlap). Naive product OVERSTATES rarity by ~1.2–1.4x for this pair.
  - Top-quintile attractiveness ↔ Cool Girl Agreeableness: positive halo (r ≈ 0.10–0.15). OVERSTATES by ~1.2x.
  - Top-quintile attractiveness ↔ low neuroticism: positive halo (r ≈ 0.10–0.15). OVERSTATES by ~1.2x.
  - Cool Girl Agreeableness ↔ low neuroticism: positive (both load on stability factor; r ≈ 0.20–0.25). OVERSTATES by ~1.3x.
  - No-kids ↔ currently single: positive (~1.2x OVERSTATES).
  - Top-quintile attractiveness ↔ currently single: NEGATIVE in real world (more attractive women more likely partnered). UNDERSTATES rarity by ~1.2–1.4x.
  - Top-quintile attractiveness ↔ doesn't-follow-girl-culture: NEGATIVE in real world (Mean-Girls / popular-girl framing maps onto high mainstream-girl-culture engagement). UNDERSTATES rarity by ~1.3–1.5x — and the Tifa stack DELIBERATELY violates this real-world correlation (the male-fantasy "top-tier hot AND martial-artist-bartender-coded" combination is the same genre-violation pattern as Mikaela / Lara / Black Widow / Wonder Woman / Ciri).
  - **Net direction:** The positive halos (multiple — Cool Girl + low-N + attractiveness all cluster on the "stability + halo" factor, plus body-comp / attractiveness positive halo, plus no-kids / single positive) are numerous and meaningful. The negative correlations (attractiveness↔single, attractiveness↔not-girly) are present and the not-girly violation is flagrant for a martial-artist-bartender-eco-terrorist character. For Tifa specifically, the positive halos modestly DOMINATE because the Cool Girl + low-N + attractiveness cluster is loaded heavily and these traits genuinely co-occur in real-world data, while the attractiveness↔not-girly violation is a single (substantial) negative direction. Net adjustment: roughly **1.1–1.2x rarer** than the naive product (slight net rarity premium because the not-girly violation is real and specific to fictional male-fantasy projection, but the positive-halo cluster is structurally more numerous).
- **Adjusted estimate:** **~7.0e-6** (≈ 1 in 143,000 women aged 20–28). Bracket: [3e-6, 2e-5].
- **Population implication:** Out of ~14M US women aged 20–28, roughly **98** match Tifa Lockhart's universal-desirable stack as captured by the male-target methodology (range ~50–280 across the bracket). This places Tifa structurally in the **same band as CJ Parker (~13 matching, ticket 016) and Mikaela Banes (~24 matching, ticket 008)** — the rare-end cluster of the male-target distribution where multiple sub-0.25 filters compound. Tifa specifically lands a bit less rare than CJ Parker because Tifa lacks the high-SOI factor (CJ has the bikini-stripper-coding, while Tifa has the wholesome-childhood-friend-coding). She is dramatically rarer than Yuna (~660 matching, ticket 018) because Tifa carries the body-composition joint (0.02) that the petite-modest Yuna does not. Within the 1990s decade-slot specifically, Tifa is the **highest-stacked single character** in the dataset (Lara Croft hits ~7.7e-5 with no Cool Girl filter; CJ Parker hits ~1.1e-6 with a high-SOI factor that adds an order of magnitude; Tifa sits between them at ~7e-6). **Critically: this male-target-methodology number under-counts Tifa's total stack — see Notes / Edge Cases for the symmetric-trait-list comparison, which would migrate her substantially leftward on the rarity axis.**

## Confidence Assessment
- **Overall:** medium-high
- **Trait identification:** high — Tifa Lockhart's classic FFVII traits are exhaustively documented across Wikipedia, Final Fantasy Wiki / Fandom, the Final Fantasy VII Ultimania Omega and Final Fantasy VII 10th Anniversary Ultimania (the canonical Square Enix character compendia), the original game itself, the Compilation of FFVII tie-ins, and 25+ years of cultural-criticism coverage. Canon is unambiguous on age (20 in 1997 game), single status, no-kids, body composition (Ultimania-published 92–60–88 cm + iconically buxom 1997 polygonal model), low-neuroticism characterization (Lifestream sequence + decades of Cool Girl coding), tomboy-coded martial-artist-bartender interest profile, and Cool Girl Agreeableness (one of the most-cited Cool Girl exemplars in JRPG criticism).
- **Base rates:** medium-high — all seven base rates used are already in `base-rates.md` from prior tickets, with strong NHANES / Pew / Census / Big-5 / bra-fit / OkCupid provenance. The body-composition joint (0.02 for BMI 18.5–22 AND DD+) is a composite estimate rather than a single-source measurement, which is the lowest-confidence base rate in the stack — same caveat as CJ Parker (ticket 016) and Lara Croft (ticket 026) which used the same joint.
- **Correlation adjustments:** medium-low — the directional claim that the positive halos modestly dominate the negative correlations for the Tifa stack is well-supported in mate-market and Big-5 literature, but the magnitude (~1.1–1.2x net rarer than naive product) is judgment, not a measured joint distribution. Bracketing of [3e-6, 2e-5] is wide for that reason.
- **Methodological-asymmetry caveat:** **high relevance, medium confidence reduction.** Tifa is a master martial artist trained from age 13 in the Zangan-ryu style, with an in-game canonical role as one of the party's elite combatants. The male-target trait list captures NONE of this as a universal-desirable filter. A symmetric-trait-list analysis would migrate Tifa substantially leftward on the rarity axis (see Notes / Edge Cases). Same caveat structure as Black Widow / Wonder Woman / Ciri / Lara Croft, less pronounced than Lara Croft (whose multi-domain expert-skill stack is dataset-leading) but still meaningful.

## Notes / Edge Cases

- **Methodological asymmetry — central caveat for Tifa:** Tifa is a master martial artist (Zangan-ryu) trained from age 13 under the in-game canonical grandmaster of the style, with an explicit gameplay role as one of the party's elite physical combatants and a full set of canonical limit-break techniques (Beat Rush, Somersault, Waterkick, Meteodrive, Dolphin Blow, Meteor Strike, Final Heaven). She also runs a successful small business (Seventh Heaven) in a hostile underclass environment, raises an adopted child, and operates as a senior member of an underground freedom-fighter cell. **The male-target trait list captures NONE of these as universal-desirable filters** because the project's premise is that male-target media doesn't typically *stack* skills onto female love interests as universal-desirable sliders the way female-target media stacks them onto male leads. If a SYMMETRIC trait-list analysis were applied — counting expert-combatant ~1e-3 (per the base-rates.md "expert combatant" entry from ticket 017), top-quintile fitness ~0.20, plausibly ~0.02–0.05 IQ ≥130 implied by tactical combat + business management, family-unencumbered ~0.05 (orphaned by Sephiroth's Nibelheim massacre) — Tifa would gain roughly 4–5 additional rarity multipliers and her joint probability would collapse by **3–5 orders of magnitude** to ~1e-9 to ~1e-11. This would migrate her toward the rarest tier of the entire dataset, comparable to mid-pack female-target heartthrobs like Bridgerton-lead (ticket 011) or Edward Cullen (ticket 007). The plan's call (asymmetric trait lists) is taken because the male-target *audience* does not appear to stack those traits as universal sliders — Tifa is a male-fantasy figure first because she is hot-AND-buxom-AND-Cool-Girl-AND-childhood-friend, second because she can throw a meteor punch. The math captures the sliders that actually drive male-fantasy projection. **Flag this asymmetry in the final-graph annotations.** Same structural caveat as Black Widow / Wonder Woman / Ciri / Lara Croft — Tifa is less extreme than Lara on this axis (Lara's symmetric-analysis stack is dataset-leading) but still substantially more skill-stacked than the male-target methodology captures.

- **Body composition joint and the canonical-versions divergence:** The original 1997 Squaresoft polygonal model and the published FFVII Ultimania measurements (92–60–88 cm at 167 cm height) place Tifa unambiguously in the BMI 18.5–22 AND DD+ joint. The 2020 FFVII Remake redesign deliberately walked back the bust dimensions per producer Yoshinori Kitase's published 2020 Famitsu interview (the redesign added a sports-bra underlayer beneath the tank top and reduced the polygon-model bust dimensions to satisfy international ratings boards). The Remake Tifa would still arguably clear the joint (athletic-build with notable bust + slim waist + low BMI), but the Squaresoft 1997 reference is the load-bearing canonical version per project anchor convention and per the heartthrobs.md decade-slot for the 1990s. Same treatment as Lara Croft (ticket 026) where the Core Design 1996 anchor was preferred over the 2013 Crystal Dynamics reboot for the 1990s decade-slot. The 0.02 joint base rate from `base-rates.md` is the appropriate input.

- **Age 20 at base-population lower edge:** Tifa is canonically 20 at FFVII game start (May 1987 birth + 20 years = May 2007 main events per Ultimania Omega timeline). Sits exactly at the lower edge of the 20–28 base population. The Compilation extends her into early-mid 20s (Advent Children at ~22, Dirge of Cerberus at ~23) — all comfortably within window. No age-mismatch caveat, unlike Yuna (ticket 018) who is canonically 17–19.

- **Cool Girl reading and the Aerith comparison:** "Top-decile Agreeableness" is a within-population statistical descriptor, and Tifa's quietly-supportive-and-low-friction characterization in 1997 JRPG context maps cleanly to it. The structural counterpoint is Aerith (the alternative-route love interest in FFVII), who is more independent, more willing to push back on Cloud and the party, and less Cool-Girl-coded. The deliberate Tifa-vs-Aerith framing in FFVII writing IS the canonical Cool-Girl-vs-independent-girl axis of late-90s JRPG romance writing — and Tifa is the textbook Cool Girl side of it. This makes Tifa one of the cleanest top-decile-Agreeableness exemplars in the dataset (alongside Yuna). High confidence on the 0.10 filter.

- **Highwind scene and the single-status reading:** The optional Disc 3 Highwind scene (with high-affection or low-affection variant depending on player choices) implies but does not confirm a consummated relationship between Cloud and Tifa within the original FFVII canonical arc. The canonical reading per multiple Square Enix interviews and the Compilation tie-ins is that Cloud and Tifa's relationship deepens through the Highwind scene but remains in an ambiguous non-formal status through the original game. Per the male-fantasy filter convention (no spouse, no live-in partner, no committed romantic relationship at the moment of audience contact), Tifa cleanly clears the single filter at game start (Sector 7 bar in Midgar, no canonical partner). A reviewer who reads the post-Highwind Disc 3 status as "in committed relationship" would still apply the single filter at the moment of audience contact (game start), so the joint is robust to this ambiguity.

- **Comparison to other 1990s male-target heartthrobs in the dataset:** Tifa's adjusted joint (~7.0e-6) sits between CJ Parker (~1.1e-6, ticket 016) and Lara Croft (~7.7e-5, ticket 026) in the 1990s decade-slot. CJ is rarer because she adds the high-SOI factor (bikini-stripper coding) that wholesome-childhood-friend Tifa does not carry. Lara is less rare because she lacks the Cool Girl filter (aristocratic-aloof rather than warm-supportive). Linda Barrett (ticket 024, ~1.7e-2 / ~1 in 60) is dramatically less rare because she lacks both the body-composition joint AND the Cool Girl + low-N + not-girly cluster. The 1990s male-target distribution thus spans roughly 4 orders of magnitude across its 5 character slots — Tifa sits in the rare band.

- **Comparison to Yuna (ticket 018) — the structural twin within the Final Fantasy franchise:** Tifa and Yuna are the canonical FFVII / FFX heroines respectively, and both are male-target Cool Girl stacks.
