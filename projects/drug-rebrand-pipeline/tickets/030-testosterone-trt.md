# Ticket 030 — Anabolic steroids → Testosterone Replacement Therapy (TRT)

## Demon identity

Anabolic-androgenic steroids (AAS) — testosterone esters, nandrolone, trenbolone, oxandrolone, stanozolol, others. Cultural demonization waves: 1988 Ben Johnson Olympics scandal; 1990 Anabolic Steroid Control Act (made AAS Schedule III); 2005–2007 BALCO / baseball PED scandals (Bonds, A-Rod, Clemens, Mitchell Report 2007). "Steroids" as cultural shorthand for cheating in sports and as a "real man drug" in gym/bodybuilding subculture.

## Medicine identity

**Testosterone replacement therapy (TRT)** — prescribed testosterone (gels like AndroGel, cypionate / enanthate injections, patches, pellets). FDA-approved for primary and hypogonadotropic hypogonadism. AndroGel approved 2000 — became the marketing flagship that drove TRT prescribing growth. Clinic-based "low T" marketing (post-2010 explosion) is a distinct cultural phenomenon.

## Arc type

`street_to_medicine`. Steroid/PED cultural identity got rebranded as legitimate medicine for "low T" / age-related hypogonadism through aggressive direct-to-consumer marketing. Same molecule (testosterone) on both sides; legal vs. illegal status depends on prescription.

## Chemistry hypothesis

**Identical** for testosterone-as-PED vs. testosterone-as-TRT. Same molecule, same esters in many cases (testosterone cypionate is testosterone cypionate whether bought from a UGL or AbbVie). Other AAS molecules (nandrolone, trenbolone) are siblings/cousins to testosterone.

5. **2014 FDA safety announcement** on TRT cardiovascular concerns and subsequent labeling changes.
6. **Recent literature** on TRT clinic proliferation and "men's health" marketing.

## Caveats

- The TRT rebrand is one of the most aggressive direct-to-consumer marketing campaigns in pharma history. The "Is It Low T?" ads of the 2010s are textbook.
- The clinical legitimacy of TRT for age-related testosterone decline is genuinely contested — this is not a clean "medicine for real disease" rebrand. Honesty section critical.
- The PED community and the TRT community substantially overlap — same drugs, different access paths.
- Public awareness of "TRT is just doctor-prescribed steroids" is moderate and rising.
