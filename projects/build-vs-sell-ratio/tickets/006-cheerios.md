# Ticket 006 — Box of Cheerios

## Product

A box of Cheerios cereal.

## Parent company

## Panel

## Segment to use

**General Mills North America Retail segment.** Cheerios is one of the segment's largest brands. Use segment revenue/operating income; pull advertising from consolidated disclosures.

## Source hierarchy

1. **General Mills FY2024 10-K** (fiscal year ending May 2024). Segment reporting, advertising expense (typically disclosed for cereal/CPG), R&D.
2. **General Mills CAGNY presentation** for segment-level marketing-intensity color.
3. **Kantar Media** for Cheerios brand-specific US ad spend.
4. **IRI / Statista** for cereal category dynamics.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Cereal is in long-term volume decline; brands have responded with steady-to-rising ad intensity. Recent ratio reflects defensive marketing.
- Trade promotion is large in cereal (slotting fees at supermarket chains, end-cap displays, in-store promotions). Some flows through gross-to-net adjustments — note if you can separate.
- Distribution: General Mills self-distributes through DSD (direct store delivery) for fresh/refrigerated and warehouse delivery for cereal. Counted as build.
- Capex: meaningful (mills, packaging lines, etc.).
