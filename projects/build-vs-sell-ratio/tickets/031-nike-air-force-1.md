# Ticket 031 — Nike Air Force 1s

## Product

A pair of Nike Air Force 1 sneakers.

## Parent company

NIKE, Inc. (NYSE: NKE).

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**NIKE Brand consolidated** (excluding Converse). Nike reports by geographic segment but classifies by brand. Use NIKE Brand revenue and apply consolidated expense ratios.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Nike FY2024 10-K** (fiscal year ending May 2024) — segment reporting, demand-creation expense (Nike's term for advertising) broken out.
2. **Nike investor presentations** for color on demand-creation and DTC mix.
3. **Sports business journal** for category context.

## Specific caveats for this product

- Nike calls advertising "demand creation" and discloses it explicitly — typically 9-11% of revenue. Use the disclosed number.
- Athlete and team endorsements (LeBron, Jordan) are within demand creation — sell.
- Nike Stores and Nike Direct e-commerce — store rent → sell, e-commerce distribution → build.
- Production is contracted (Vietnam, Indonesia, China) — flows through COGS as build.
- Air Force 1 is a long-tail evergreen — much lower marketing per pair than newest releases, but allocation will use brand-average.

## Output format

`results/031-nike-air-force-1.md` per spec.

## What "good data" looks like

- Demand-creation expense cited from 10-K.
- Build_pct probably 65-80%.
- Confidence: **high**.
