## Methodology notes

**Segment selection.** Used Kenvue's Self Care reportable segment as the proxy for Tylenol's economics. Self Care is the largest of Kenvue's three segments (Self Care, Skin Health and Beauty, Essential Health) and bundles Tylenol with sibling OTC franchises Motrin, Zyrtec, Sudafed, Nicorette, Pepcid, and Imodium. Tylenol alone is reported as ~$1B+ in annual sales (high-single-digit % of consolidated Kenvue revenue per investor commentary), so the segment captures Tylenol plus a coherent cluster of similar OTC-pharmacy brands with broadly similar build/sell economics. Segment net sales ($6,527M) and segment adjusted operating income ($2,173M) are taken directly from the FY2024 earnings release and 10-K.

**Parent-level cost allocation.** Kenvue discloses cost of sales ($6,496M), SG&A ($6,329M), and R&D ($408M, embedded in SG&A) at the consolidated level only. Total advertising / brand investment is not broken out in the 10-K as a single line — Kenvue treats brand investment as part of SG&A — but the company has publicly indicated 2024 ad spend ran ~$1.6B (about 10–11% of revenue, up from 8.7% in 2023, per MediaPost reporting that quotes Kenvue management). Allocated all four lines to Self Care via the segment's revenue share (6,527 / 15,455 = 42.23%), per the plan.md fallback when brand-level data is undisclosed.

**Profit anchor — adjusted to GAAP-allocated.** Self Care segment adjusted operating income of $2,173M is the cleanest direct disclosure, but Kenvue's adjusted OI excludes a lot — depreciation ($329M), corporate unallocated ($314M), amortization of intangibles ($269M), separation-related costs ($296M), restructuring/Vue Forward ($221M), impairment charges ($578M, mostly the Dr.Ci:Labo® write-down which actually sits in Skin Health and Beauty), founder shares ($29M), stock-based award conversion ($39M), and other items. Total of these below-segment deductions is ~$2,101M for FY24. Allocating Self Care's pro-rata share of these (42.23% × $2,101M = $887M) brings Self Care's GAAP-equivalent operating income down from $2,173M to $1,286M — that is the profit number used here. This avoids over-stating Self Care's profit by treating the segment as if it bore none of Kenvue's corporate, separation, or impairment costs. Note: the Dr.Ci:Labo® impairment is genuinely a Skin Health and Beauty event, so allocating its $578M pro-rata to Self Care arguably over-burdens Self Care — but the alternative (zero impairment for Self Care) under-burdens it for the corporate and separation costs that do touch the whole company. Pro-rata is the middle path.

**Segment-cost residual classification.** Self Care's segment-level operating costs (segment net sales minus segment adjusted OI) total $4,354M. After subtracting the pro-rata COGS ($2,743M), R&D ($172M), and advertising ($684M), the residual segment SG&A is $755M — covering segment-allocated selling expenses, distribution overlay, sales-force, trade promotion, segment admin, and other operating items. Split 50/50 between sell-like ($377M) and overhead-like ($377M) per the plan.md convention. A different split inside the conventional 40–60% band would shift build_pct by ~±1 percentage point.

**Recent spinoff caveat.** Kenvue spun off from Johnson & Johnson in May 2023, so FY2024 is only the second full year as a standalone company. Reported financials carry $296M of separation-related costs, ~$185M of restructuring under the Vue Forward optimization program, and the Dr.Ci:Labo® intangible impairment ($578M, mostly Q2 2024). These will normalize down materially in 2025+ — meaning today's GAAP profit number understates steady-state profit by several hundred million. Build_pct is fairly insensitive to this (the split between build and sell doesn't change), but the absolute profit slice in any visualization will look smaller than Kenvue's long-run earnings power. Flagging because the post-spinoff cost noise is a known feature of this name.

**Trade promotion / pharmacy rebates.** Per CPG and OTC accounting practice, trade promotion, slotting fees, and pharmacy / wholesaler rebates are typically netted against revenue rather than booked in SG&A. Reported segment net sales of $6,527M is therefore net-of-promo, and the sell number here understates gross-of-promo selling spend consistently — does not move the ratio meaningfully.

**Advertising-intensity uncertainty (key sensitivity).** Self Care's brands (Tylenol, Motrin, Zyrtec, Sudafed, Nicorette) are heavy direct-to-consumer advertisers — Tylenol in particular has run continuous trust-restoration marketing since the 1982 Chicago tampering crisis and the 2010 manufacturing recalls. So pro-rata advertising of $684M / $6,527M = 10.5% of segment revenue may slightly understate Self Care's true ad share if the segment over-indexes vs. the parent average. Counter-balancing factor: Skin Health and Beauty (Neutrogena, Aveeno) and Essential Health (Listerine, Band-Aid) are also heavy advertisers, so the consolidated 10–11% ratio probably isn't far off Self Care's actual. If Self Care actually ran 12–13% ad-to-sales, sell would be ~$100–150M higher and build_pct would drop ~1.5–2 percentage points (toward 71–72%). Did not pull Kantar / Ad Age's brand-level Tylenol-only number; flagging as the largest single uncertainty.

**Confidence: medium.** Segment net sales and segment adjusted operating income come directly from a US 10-K and earnings release (high confidence). Build/sell allocation requires (a) pro-rating consolidated COGS, R&D, and advertising by segment revenue share, (b) allocating Self Care's share of below-segment items — separation, restructuring, impairment, etc. — that won't all recur in steady-state, and (c) splitting residual SG&A 50/50. Each step is defensible but involves estimation. Kenvue's recent-spinoff status amplifies noise vs. mature CPG comparables. Calling this "high" would overclaim the precision of brand-level OTC ad disclosure.
