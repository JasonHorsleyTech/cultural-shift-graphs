# Ticket 015 — Thalidomide → Thalomid

## Demon identity

**Thalidomide.** Marketed 1957–1962 (Grünenthal in Germany, brand names Contergan, Distaval, others) as a sedative and morning-sickness drug. Caused an estimated 10,000+ births with severe limb deformities (phocomelia) globally. Pulled from market 1961–1962 country-by-country. The international symbol of pharma malpractice — drove the 1962 Kefauver-Harris Amendment in the US (strengthened FDA efficacy review). Never approved by the FDA for the morning-sickness indication (Frances Kelsey's blocking of the application is a foundational FDA history moment).

## Medicine identity

**Thalomid** (thalidomide, same molecule). FDA approval for cutaneous manifestations of erythema nodosum leprosum (ENL) in 1998. Subsequent off-label and supplemental approval for multiple myeloma (FDA approved 2006 for newly diagnosed MM in combination with dexamethasone). Celgene (now BMS). Plus the better-known **lenalidomide (Revlimid)** and **pomalidomide (Pomalyst)** — chemical descendants of thalidomide, far more commercially significant. Black-box pregnancy warnings; access controlled via the THALOMID REMS (formerly STEPS) program.

## Arc type

`failed_medicine_rebranded`. The textbook case of the project's second arc. Same molecule, completely different indication, decades-later relaunch. Not a street drug at any point — purely medicine-to-disaster-to-medicine.

## Chemistry hypothesis

**Identical** for the Thalomid relaunch. The molecule is thalidomide both times. The pregnancy controls (REMS) are the only meaningful difference between 1961 and 2006 deployment.

For lenalidomide (Revlimid) and pomalidomide: **sibling** — thalidomide analogs, structurally close, same immunomodulatory mechanism. Worth a brief mention as the commercial-success continuation of the rebrand.

## Caveats

- The Thalomid rebrand is *unique* in that they kept a name that explicitly invokes the disaster ("Thalidomide" is right there in "Thalomid"). Most failed-medicine rebrands use new names (Bupropion → Zyban). Worth flagging — Celgene's bet was that with REMS controls, transparency about the molecule was a feature not a bug.
- Lenalidomide / pomalidomide are commercially much bigger than Thalomid itself. The rebrand's full impact is in the analog family, not the original molecule.
- Public awareness of the thalidomide-myeloma connection is probably low except among oncology-adjacent people. Find survey data if any exists.
- The morning-sickness-to-cancer indication switch is the project's wildest "different problem entirely" rebrand.
