# Raiden Shogun (Ei)

## Character Summary
- **Source work:** Genshin Impact (HoYoverse / Cognosphere, action-RPG / gacha launched 2020). The Raiden Shogun (true name **Ei**) was added as a playable five-star character in Version 2.1 (released 1 September 2021), introduced narratively as the central antagonist-then-ally figure of the Inazuma Archon Quest (Version 2.0–2.1, July–September 2021). Ei is the Electro Archon, ruler of Inazuma (the game's Japanese-inspired island nation), one of the Seven Archons of Teyvat.
- **Audience:** male-targeted
- **Decade slot:** 2020s
- **Cultural reach proxy:** Genshin Impact has generated approximately **$5.4+ billion in mobile revenue** since launch (Sensor Tower / AppMagic cumulative reporting through 2024) — one of the highest-grossing free-to-play games in history. Peak monthly active users exceeded 60 million globally; cumulative downloads (mobile + console + PC) exceed 200 million. Raiden Shogun was **the highest-grossing five-star banner of 2021** at her release, generating roughly $260M+ in her debut three weeks per Sensor Tower revenue tracking — at the time, the largest single-character revenue event in mobile gaming history. She has remained one of the top tier Genshin characters in popularity polls (HoYoverse official polls, Reddit r/Genshin_Impact community polls, IGN / GameRant / GameSpot character rankings 2021–2025) and has consistently appeared in "best video game waifu" / "hottest female video game characters" / "most iconic Genshin characters" rankings throughout the 2020s. Continuous top-tier cosplay status at conventions 2021–present (San Diego Comic-Con, Anime Expo, China Joy, similar global anime/gaming conventions). Her musical theme "The Almighty Shogun" has been performed by orchestras globally as part of the Genshin Concert tour series.
- **Canonical version used:** Per ticket instruction, the **Raiden Shogun (Ei)** as introduced in Genshin Impact Version 2.0–2.1 (July–September 2021), the load-bearing iconic version of the character. Per franchise canon (Genshin Impact Wiki / Fandom, Ei's character profile, Inazuma Archon Quest dialogue, Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter / Story Quest material): **Ei** is one of the two Raiden twins — her elder sister Makoto was the original Electro Archon (Baal), who died approximately 500 years before the present-day setting during the cataclysm. Following her sister's death, Ei (originally "Beelzebul") created a **puppet — the Raiden Shogun** — to rule Inazuma in the public-facing Archon role while she withdrew her consciousness into the Plane of Euthymia, a meditative dimension where she pursued her ideal of "Eternity." The Vision Hunt Decree (the load-bearing political plot of the Inazuma quest) was the puppet Shogun's autonomous policy executed without Ei's direct intervention. Following the player-character (Traveler)'s defeat of the puppet Shogun and confrontation with Ei in the Plane of Euthymia, she emerges from her meditative withdrawal and begins to rule more compassionately. Visual canon: tall (Genshin's "Tall Female" body type, ~5'7"–5'8" implied scale), straight long purple hair tied in a ponytail with crystal pin, traditional Japanese-Inazuman armored kimono, signature Musou Isshin / Musou no Hitotachi greatsword (weapon she manifests during her Elemental Burst). Voice cast (English): Ana Yi Puig; (Japanese): Miyuki Sawashiro; (Mandarin): Ying Xu; (Korean): Yoo Hae-jeong.

- **Trait identification:** high — Raiden Shogun's traits across the canonical 2.0/2.1 reference window are extensively documented (Wikipedia, Genshin Impact Wiki / Fandom, Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter Story Quest dialogue, Inazuma Archon Quest dialogue, primary source via gameplay, HoYoverse official character profile, voice cast interviews, four-plus years of game press coverage). - **Base rates:** high — all six base rates used (top-quintile attractiveness, BMI 18.5–22 marginal, currently single, no kids, low neuroticism, doesn't-follow-girl-culture) are already in `base-rates.md` from prior tickets, with strong NHANES / Pew / Census / Big-5 / OkCupid / Langlois-meta-analysis / Nielsen provenance. No new entries required for this ticket.
- **Correlation adjustments:** medium-low — the directional claim that the fictional Raiden stack violates real-world negative correlations between attractiveness and tomboy/warrior-coded interests is well-supported in mate-market literature, but the exact magnitude (~neutral-to-1.2x adjustment) is judgment, not a measured joint distribution. Bracketing of [4e-4, 2e-3] is wide for that reason.
- **Cool Girl exclusion:** high confidence in the NOT-counted call. Raiden's dignified-formal-Archon characterization is one of the cleanest counter-examples to warm-supportive Cool Girl coding in the dataset — she is *formally distant* about NOT being warm-low-friction-supportive, structurally aligned with Lara Croft / Selene / Daenerys / Bayonetta rather than with Yuna / Black Widow / Wonder Woman.
- **High-SOI exclusion:** high confidence. Raiden's canonical asexual-functioning regal-stoic characterization is unambiguous — there is no canonical sexual content or performative sexual coding, and her load-bearing emotional energy is directed at her late sister and her duty as Archon rather than at any romantic / sexual register. Same exclusion as Yuna / Selene / Wonder Woman / Lara Croft / 2B.
- **DD+ exclusion / BMI marginal call:** medium-high confidence. Genshin's Tall Female body type carries a moderate bust line in splash art, but Raiden's design lacks the documented designer-intent buxom emphasis of Tifa (Tetsuya Nomura) / 2B (Yoko Taro) and the exaggerated 1.5+ bust-to-waist ratio of CJ Parker / Jessica Rabbit. A reviewer who insisted on the BMI+DD+ joint (treating Genshin's Tall Female body type as buxom-coded) would pull the joint to ~5.9e-5 (~1 in 17,000), placing Raiden in 2B's band rather than Selene's. - **Age-window framing:** medium-low confidence reduction. Raiden is canonically thousands of years old (Ei is implied to predate the cataclysm 500 years before present, and her divine origin is older still). Per dataset convention (Selene 620 years, Bayonetta 600 years, Wonder Woman immortal, Wanda, 2B), the joint is computed against the 20–28 base anyway because the male-fantasy projection treats her as the archetypal young-adult-woman regardless of canonical age. Same caveat structure as those tickets.
- **Methodological-asymmetry caveat:** **high relevance, medium confidence reduction.** Raiden Shogun is an Archon (god-tier divine ruler), Musou Isshin swordmaster (master close-combatant + master swordsmith), dimensional-manipulator (Plane of Euthymia control + cross-dimensional consciousness transfer), governs an entire nation, has accumulated ~thousands of years of experience, and possesses Archon-tier supernatural physical capabilities. The male-target trait list captures NONE of this as universal-desirable filters. A symmetric-trait-list analysis would migrate Raiden substantially leftward on the rarity axis (see Notes / Edge Cases).
