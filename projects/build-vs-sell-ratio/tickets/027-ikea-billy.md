# Ticket 027 — IKEA Billy bookcase

## Product

IKEA Billy bookcase (the iconic flat-pack laminate bookcase).

## Parent company

Inter IKEA Group (private; Netherlands-headquartered franchise owner). Note: Ingka Group operates most retail stores under franchise.

## Panel

consumer

## Segment to use

**Inter IKEA Group consolidated.** Inter IKEA owns the brand and supply chain; Ingka owns retail stores (most of them). For our rubric, the Inter IKEA Group financials are the closest analog to a parent company because they capture the design-and-supply-chain function. **Note explicitly:** retail store ops (Ingka) are at a separate entity. This is similar to the Coca-Cola bottler issue — flag it.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Inter IKEA Group Annual Summary FY2024** (fiscal year ending August 2024) — IKEA voluntarily publishes a financial summary.
2. **Ingka Group Annual Summary FY2024** — separate filing for the retail entity. Useful as context for the full chain.
3. **IKEA Annual Sustainability Report** for some operational color.
4. **Trade press** for category context.

## Specific caveats for this product

- IKEA is structurally split — Inter IKEA (brand/supply) and Ingka (retail) — both publish data. Default approach: use Inter IKEA Group as the "parent" and note that retail store operations sit at a separate legal entity.
- Or alternatively: combine both for a "full IKEA" view (sum revenue, sum operating costs at appropriate level) — this gives a more honest view of the consumer-facing economics. Note approach explicitly.
- IKEA stores ARE retail real estate per rubric → sell. But IKEA stores are also functional (try-the-furniture, eat-the-meatballs). Same gray zone as Apple Stores.
- Marketing for IKEA includes the iconic catalog (now discontinued post-2020) and digital — meaningful but not as heavy as luxury.
- Capex is significant — distribution centers, store buildings (owned in many markets).

`results/027-ikea-billy.md` per spec. Methodology must explain Inter IKEA vs. Ingka structure.

## What "good data" looks like

- Both filings cited; the entity boundary explicitly chosen and explained.
- Build_pct probably 70-85% if measured at Inter IKEA level (mostly supply chain), lower if combined with Ingka retail.
- Confidence: **medium**.
